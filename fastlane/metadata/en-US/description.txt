NOOR is a calm, focused companion for your daily Islamic remembrance — built for Muslims who want to stay connected to Allah without distractions.

DHIKR COUNTER
Tap to count Subhan Allah, Alhamdulillah, Allahu Akbar, and more. Gold ring fills as you reach your goal (33 or 99). Your count is saved across sessions.

DUA LIBRARY
Curated duas for anxiety, gratitude, morning, evening, travel, and more. Arabic text, transliteration, and meaning — all offline.

DAILY REFLECTION
A private space to write thoughts, intentions, and gratitude. Completely local — nothing leaves your device.

WINS TRACKER
Note your small and big victories each day. Build momentum. See your growth.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account. No cloud sync. No tracking. Everything stays on your iPhone.
